About M&M Mobile Grid

Made with love, exclusively for my beloved Marjorie B.

Create photo collages, add text and image overlays, edit each photo, and save or share your grid.

What’s new

  • Mobile bottom editor with grouped photo tools and a canvas that stays visible.
  • Photos and Save image are always available at the top on mobile.
  • Hamburger menu with Settings and About & updates.
  • Per-photo filters, exposure, highlights, shadows, warmth, tint, vibrance, sharpening, and vignette.
  • Golden Hour, Light & Airy, Cinematic, Matte Film, and Dramatic Mono presets.
  • Drag to crop, pinch to zoom, reorder photos, and hold to compare edits with the original.
  • Aesthetic text fonts, opacity controls, and improved mobile color controls.
  • Save directly from the canvas with sharing and error notifications.

Saving on iPhone

Tap Save image, then choose Save Image in the phone’s share sheet. The app confirms sharing completed; your phone handles saving to Photos.

Photos are edited on your device. Saved templates stay in this browser.

Save your image

On iPhone, tap Share / Save to Photos and choose Save Image. You can also touch and hold the preview to save it. On Android, use the share sheet or download the image.
